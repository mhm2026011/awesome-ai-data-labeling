# Datasets for AI-Assisted Data Labeling

Below is the detailed list of datasets used to benchmark and evaluate AI-assisted versus manual data labeling across scientific domains.

1. **Tabula Sapiens**  
   *Source:* Chan Zuckerberg Biohub  
   *Description:* A massive multi-organ single-cell transcriptomic atlas containing over nearly 500,000 cells.  
   *Use Case:* Benchmarking AI-assisted and LLM-driven *de novo* cell-type annotation accuracy.  
   *Link:* [Tabula Sapiens Consortium](https://tabula-sapiens-portal.ds.czbiohub.org/)

2. **BraTS 2023 (Brain Tumor Segmentation)**  
   *Source:* RSNA / MICCAI / Synapse  
   *Description:* Multi-institutional MRI scans of adult glioma and pediatric brain tumors with expert consensus annotations.  
   *Use Case:* Evaluating spatial overlap metrics (Dice/IoU) of AI-generated boundaries against human baseline masks.  
   *Link:* [BraTS 2023 Challenge - Synapse](https://www.synapse.org/brats2023)

3. **Galaxy Zoo (LSST/Euclid Data)**  
   *Source:* Zooniverse  
   *Description:* Massive crowdsourced morphological classifications of spiral, elliptical, and merging galaxies.  
   *Use Case:* Training zero-shot foundation models and Vision-Language Models (VLMs) for morphological anomaly detection.  
   *Link:* [Galaxy Zoo](https://www.zooniverse.org/projects/zookeeper/galaxy-zoo/)
